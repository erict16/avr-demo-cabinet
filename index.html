Before building

  • Confirm the junction box size (300 × 400 × 150 assumed)
  • Confirm the BCD box size and the W3 control cores
  • Lower the tray to 1.0–1.1 m
  • Heaviest unit at the bottom
  • Leave 100 mm behind each unit
  • RCD and earth stud on the frame
